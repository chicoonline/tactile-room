Vibration check

One minute, to find out whether this phone can vibrate from a web page, and if not, why.

Do this, in this order

  1. Before tapping anything else, drag your finger around the box below.
  2. Tap “Vibrate for half a second”.
  3. Tap “Short pulses”.
  4. Drag in the box again.
  5. Tap “Copy results” and send them to Chico.

“Accepted”, but you felt nothing?

Then the browser asked the phone to vibrate and the phone didn't. Check:

  • Silent mode and Do Not Disturb are off.
  • Battery saver is off.
  • Settings → Sound and vibration → Vibration (and touch feedback) are on, and not at zero.
  • You're in Chrome or Samsung Internet. Firefox and iPhones can't vibrate from a web page.
