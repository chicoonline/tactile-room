Tactile Room

Explore objects through sound and vibration, with or without seeing them. The first is a sea snail shell: press and drag across it, or select it and use the arrow keys.

Higher notes where the shell sticks out. A brighter, crackly sound over ridges. A short falling sound when you leave the shell. Two fingers turn it (on a screen or a trackpad), or right-drag with a mouse; pinch to zoom. The page doesn't scroll while you touch this area: scroll outside it.

Gamepad
Left stick moves your fingertip (hold the left trigger for small steps), right stick turns the shell, A or ✕ describes the spot, B or ○ plays the description, Y or △ goes back to the middle

The shell vibrates under your finger on Android phones and with a gamepad. Choose Strong if you feel nothing; older phones need longer pulses. iPhones can’t vibrate from a web page.
